For parents & teachers

A full-year sixth-grade science study app based on the topics in the St. Johns 2026–27 recommended guide and Florida benchmarks. Your teacher’s pacing and test details remain the best guide for your class.

Five independent paths cover inquiry/physics, Earth/weather, Earth’s surface, cells, and living systems. Start cells immediately or start the first lesson in any other unit.

Lessons unlock at 4/5 quiz answers correct. Unit challenges and the 25-question year review require at least 90% (rounded up to a whole question). All questions are original practice items, not official test questions. Florida’s statewide science assessment is in grades 5 and 8; this app builds grade 6 foundations.

Highest scores and progress save only in this browser. Devices do not sync. Private browsing or clearing site data can erase progress. No names or email addresses are collected. If storage is unavailable, the app will say so.

Sources: Florida CPALMS • Science course; CPALMS • Life science; OpenStax • Cells; OpenStax • Passive transport; FDOE • Grade 8 assessment specifications.

Diagrams and simulations are simplified teaching models. At-home activities should follow the adult-supervision notes. Pip is a built-in guide, not a live AI chat service.
